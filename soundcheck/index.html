How to play
  • Each day's recording layers four everyday sounds in an 8-second loop. Replay it as often as you like.
  • Search the library and tap the sound you think you hear. Right answers open a channel. Your first three wrong answers cost 5 points each, and every wrong answer after that costs 10, so listen before you guess.
  • Mute solved channels (free) to hear what's left underneath.
  • Focus (−10) brings one unsolved sound forward for a full loop.
  • You start with 100. The round ends when all four are solved or you reveal the answers — each sound still unsolved at reveal costs 25.
🎧 Headphones help, but a phone speaker works fine. On iPhone, flip off silent mode if you hear nothing.
