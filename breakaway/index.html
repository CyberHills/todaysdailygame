The red line sweeps across the net. Tap to lock it, then tap again to pick the height and shoot.

How to play
  • You get five shots on the same goalie. Score as many as you can.
  • First tap locks where across the net you'll shoot. Second tap locks the height and releases the shot.
  • The goalie shuffles, drops into the butterfly and moves his glove and blocker while you line up. Shoot where he isn't.
  • He reacts once you shoot. Corners far from his glove and blocker are hardest for him to reach.
  • Shots outside the posts or over the bar miss. Hitting the post rings, but it doesn't count.
  • Read the scouting report: every goalie has a weak spot.
