      Solve today's jobsite

      1. Read the job card. How much soil, the deadline, the budget, how fussy the lawn is, the soil type, and any marked utility lines.
      2. Pick your setup: something to dig with, something to move the soil, and a crew of 1–3.
      3. Choose where the machine sits if there's a choice. The blue ring is its reach; the orange ring is its rear swing — anything inside it gets hit.
      4. Check the yard. The crew's route from the trailer is drawn for you; red means a machine won't fit through. Painted lines are buried utilities: machines stay 2 ft off them, so that strip is dug by shovel. Hatched ground is a slope.
      5. Start the job and watch it play out. You get one scored run a day; practise as much as you like afterwards.

      Score: completion 40 · site protection 25 · schedule 20 (−1 for every 5 min late) · cost 15 (full marks on budget, zero at 20% over). Finishing early or under budget earns nothing extra.
