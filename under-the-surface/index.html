Field instructions

Choose where to excavate

  1. A small patch of ground hides one object. The survey team has opened one square for you (marked 0) — and it's never the giveaway part.
  2. Tap a square to brush it. You have 10 squares a day.
  3. Compare what you uncover with the sketches in your notebook. The object may be turned or flipped in the ground — use Turn and Flip to line the sketches up.
  4. Identify it whenever you're sure. A wrong identification costs 3 squares, so guessing blind won't save you.
  5. The fewer squares you use, the better. Every find you identify goes into your collection.

Tip: brush where the possibilities differ — a handle, a hole, a hub — not just next to what you've already found.
