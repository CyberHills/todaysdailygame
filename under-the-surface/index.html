Field instructions

Choose where to excavate

  1. A small patch of ground hides one object. The survey team has already opened one square (marked 0).
  2. Tap a square to brush it. You have 10 squares a day.
  3. Compare what you uncover with the sketches in your notebook. The object may be turned or flipped.
  4. Identify it whenever you're sure. A wrong identification costs 2 squares.
  5. The fewer squares you use, the better. Every find you identify goes into your collection.

Tip: brush where the possibilities differ — a handle, a hole, a hub — not where they all look the same.
