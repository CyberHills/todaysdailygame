How to play

Three questions, four choices each. Pick your answers, then back them with 10 confidence chips.

  • Every question needs at least 1 chip, and no more than 6.
  • Right answer: +10 per chip. Wrong answer: −10 per chip.
  • Change anything you like — then lock all three in together.

Scores run from −100 to +100. A new puzzle every day at midnight. Over time you'll see how often your confident answers are actually right.
