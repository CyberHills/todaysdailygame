Out of Place
Practice scene — doesn't count toward your streak.

Three things don't belong in this year. Tap them to mark them.

Magnified · drag to look around
Objects are numbered left to right. Arrow keys move, Enter or Space marks or unmarks, L locks in your three. After locking in, Enter reads the note for the highlighted object.

Other scenes

How it works. Every scene has exactly three things that didn't exist yet at that date. Rare isn't wrong: a gadget that was new or expensive at the time still belongs. Pick all three before you get any feedback. One point each, no speed bonus; a perfect 3/3 keeps your streak alive. After you lock in, tap anything in the scene to read why it does or doesn't belong. On a keyboard, click or Tab into the scene, then use the arrow keys and Enter (L locks in).
