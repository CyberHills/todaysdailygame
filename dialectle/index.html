Dialectle.

/daɪ.əˈlɛk.təl/ One word, many countries. Pick what it means in each.

How to play
  1. Each day there are five words per language, the same for everyone.
  2. For every country listed, pick what a local would mean by the word. Some meanings are decoys that no country uses.
  3. Many words share a meaning everywhere (monkey, footwear). Pick the meaning that is specific to that country.
  4. You get a point for each country you get right. Share your grid when you're done.
