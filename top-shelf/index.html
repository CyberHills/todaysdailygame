How to play
  • Each drill is like a golf hole: score in as few shots as you can. Your total is measured against par.
  • Tap, click or press Space to lock the sweeping aim line, then again to stop the power meter.
  • The puck bounces off the boards, defenders and the goalie. From period 2 the defenders skate back and forth. The goalie keeps moving while you aim and covers the middle of the net, so aim for the side he is leaving.
  • A shot that leaves the zone over the blue line costs a one-shot penalty and the puck comes back.
  • If the puck stops in the crease, the goalie covers it and play restarts at the nearest faceoff dot.
  • Slush patches slow the puck down. After par + 4 shots the drill ends.
  • Everyone gets the same nine drills and ice conditions each day, which change at midnight your local time.
